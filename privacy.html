Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how clipfarm and the clipfarm-poster application (the "Service") handle information.

1. What we process

Video files and audio: processed locally on the operator's machine to generate clips. Source media is not uploaded to any server operated by us. TikTok account data: when the operator connects a TikTok account, we receive the basic profile identifier and authorization tokens provided by TikTok Login Kit, used solely to publish clips at the operator's direction.

2. Third-party services

Publishing uses TikTok's Content Posting API under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. Authorization flows may be facilitated by Composio (composio.dev).

3. Data retention

Media stays on the operator's machine until deleted by the operator. TikTok tokens are stored by the automation layer and can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app in TikTok settings.

4. No sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Contact

Contact the repository owner via GitHub for any privacy questions or requests.
